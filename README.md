> Your behavior is your password.

## What is BioAuth?

BioAuth is a passwordless authentication system that verifies your identity through **keystroke dynamics** and **mouse movement patterns** — no passwords, just you.

---

## Features

- Keystroke & mouse biometric capture
- Real-time Trust Score (0–100%)
- Anomaly detection with OTP step-up via email
- Security dashboard with live charts & session logs
- Fully client-side — no backend needed

---

## Tech Stack

- HTML, CSS, Vanilla JavaScript
- Chart.js — trust score timeline
- EmailJS — OTP delivery

---


## Demo Steps

1. Enter your email and type the passphrase: `BioAuth@Secure#9`
2. Click **Enroll Biometric Profile**
3. Type again to **Authenticate**
4. Open the **Dashboard** to monitor your trust score in real time
